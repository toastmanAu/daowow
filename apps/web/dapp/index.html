Everyone gets their deposit back

How the savings pool works

Everyone's ticket money is pooled and placed in the Nervos DAO, a savings feature built into the Nervos network. While it sits there it earns interest.

When the round ends, every player gets their whole deposit back, win or lose. The interest the pool earned becomes the prizes.

How long it takes

You buy tickets while entries are open. Winners are drawn soon after entries close. Your deposit then stays in savings for the term, and comes back with any prize at payout.

What it costs

Nothing is spent except a small network fee. Everything else is a deposit that is returned to you.

What you can win

The more tickets a round sells, the more prize places it pays. The prize table is fixed when the round is created and nobody can change it afterwards.
