Before we go live

Beautiful on the surface.
Clear about what’s underneath.

What can I do in this preview?

Change ticket quantities and review sample principal amounts. The sample price is 100 CKB; no live round, prize pool, odds or deadline has been loaded. Nothing is saved or sent.

Can I connect a wallet or enter yet?

Not here yet. Live round discovery, wallet integration, transaction building and confirmation tracking still need to be connected and verified. Never send CKB to an address from an unverified preview.

What should I know about my funds?

Accepted funds are locked for the round’s DAO term. Excluded entries use the protocol’s deadline-based refund path. Principal return depends on valid settlement; fees, smart-contract risk and CKB price risk still apply. This is not a guaranteed-return product.
